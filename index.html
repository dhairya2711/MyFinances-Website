Designed around real financial life

My Finances separates cash, debt, transfers, investments and market movement so the dashboard remains useful without blurring fundamentally different kinds of money.

01

Accounts and activity

Track chequing, savings, cash, credit cards, loans and investment accounts. Record income, expenses, transfers and card payments with exact-cent calculations and recoverable Trash.

02

Plans that reconcile

Create budgets, goals, recurring entries, daily reviews and payment reminders. Expected activity stays separate from posted activity until it is actually recorded.

03

Investments and currencies

Keep contributions, withdrawals, trades, holdings, prices, foreign exchange and market growth distinct. Canadian registered-account room is presented as an estimate—not a CRA record.

04

Reviewed document imports

Import CSV, XLSX and PDF statements or scan receipt photos. Extraction results remain editable and nothing enters the financial ledger until you approve it.

05

Reports you can keep

Generate complete overviews, spending analyses and investment reports as responsive, standalone HTML. Save report history, export files and configure local report alerts.

A bounded finance assistant

Ask questions, find activity or prepare supported changes. Deterministic tools calculate totals, financial writes remain reviewable, and the core app continues to work without an AI model.

Clear data boundaries

Local by default. Connected when you decide.

The app does not operate a developer-controlled finance server. Features that leave the device are separated so you can understand and choose each one.

1

On your device

Room-backed financial records, preferences, generated reports, receipt and statement extraction, deterministic calculations, and downloaded local AI models remain on the Android device.

2

In your Google account

If you connect Google, My Finances can create and manage its own Sheets workbook and Drive folders for structured synchronization, backups, reports and originals you explicitly upload.

3

With an AI provider

Cloud AI is off by default. If you add your own provider credential and select that provider, assistant requests and limited relevant context go directly to that provider under its terms.

Optional Google connection

Why Google access may be requested

Google is used only after you choose to connect it. The app requests basic account identity and the narrow drive.file permission—not unrestricted access to your Drive.

  • Confirm the connected account. The app reads the selected account’s basic name, email address and stable Google identifier so it can show which account is connected.
  • Synchronize readable records. The app creates or reuses a private My Finances workbook and uses stable IDs, validation and an explicit review for conflicting Sheet edits.
  • Create recovery copies. Manual and limited automatic backup packages are stored in app-created Drive folders. Packages contain financial records and metadata, not cloud-AI keys or local model files.
  • Store originals only on request. A receipt or statement original stays local unless you explicitly choose its Drive upload action.
  • Disconnect without deleting your files. Disconnecting revokes the app’s access and clears local connection metadata; existing Sheets and Drive files remain in your Google account for you to manage.

Practical privacy controls

Useful with no account and no model

Accounts, transactions, plans, investments, document extraction and deterministic reports work without connecting Google or an online AI provider.

  • On-device document processing. Receipt photos and scanned PDFs use on-device text recognition and local parsing. Results are suggestions until approved.
  • Optional local models. Model weights are not bundled with the app. You choose whether to download a compatible model; downloaded files are integrity-checked and removable.
  • Approval before financial writes. AI can interpret a request, but the exact-cent ledger and validation rules remain authoritative.
  • Explicit export and recovery. Create portable backup packages or standalone HTML reports when you choose. Android system backup is disabled for app financial data.
Important: My Finances is an organization and tracking tool. It does not connect to banks, move money, or provide professional financial, investment, tax, accounting or legal advice. Forecasts, category suggestions, registered-account room and AI responses can be incomplete or wrong and should be independently reviewed.
